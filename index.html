About Us

The Motion Picture Club is a community within Lawrence University dedicated to film curation and appreciation. We host weekly screenings, discussions in the on-campus cinema, and a wide range of cinema-related events for the Lawrence community.

It was founded in 2024 by freshmen Gabriel Garcia and Fizzy Williams, with the goal of expanding cinema appreciation within Lawrence University.

LU Motion Picture Film Festival

Founded in 2025, the LU Motion Picture Film Festival gives students the opportunity to gain experience participating in a professional-style film festival environment.
