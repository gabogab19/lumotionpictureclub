Victoria Ratermanis is a lifetime member of the Actor’s Studio and is currently an Oscar nominated actor for her role in the short film ‘A Lien’. She is also a writer, director and theater maker, with her premiere short film “DISC” starring Jim Cummings and herself in post, will be ready to hit the festival circuit in 2026. Born in Baltimore, and brought up overseas, her childhood as a perpetual new kid taught her adaptability and strengthened her imagination, and now she's made a career of the tendency. She is Meisner trained thanks to Playhouse West Brooklyn, and graduated with a BFA in acting from Pace University.
